A LITTLE ABOUT ME / 02

HR®
DESIGN & TECHNOLOGY EST. LEARNER

"Design is the intentional bridge between what an idea is and how people experience it."

Curious mind.
Creative eye.

I am a creative learner interested in graphic design, digital experiences, and building useful products. I enjoy exploring visual communication, brand identity, and the intersection of design and technology.

My design approach starts with curiosity: understanding the message, finding an authentic visual direction, and building a clear, memorable outcome. Rather than relying on superficial trends, I focus on typographic hierarchy, deliberate color harmony, and functional layout systems.

Coming from a Computer Science background, I bring an analytical perspective to creative work — appreciating both the artistry of typography and the systematic structure required to execute modern digital experiences.
